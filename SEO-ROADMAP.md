# TrueCraft Home Repair: local SEO plan

No ranking is promised here, and no search-volume figures are quoted. The keyword
tool account was out of credits during the build, so the targets below are
informed hypotheses from reading live Spokane results, not measured demand. Month
one fixes that.

## What the search results looked like (October 2026)

- **Carpet repair / stretching in Spokane.** Page one is mostly lead-gen
  directories (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Porch) and a couple of long-established flooring
  stores. No local business has a focused page on **pet-damaged carpet repair**.
  That is the clearest opening and it lines up with Matt's real specialty.
- **Flooring installation in Spokane.** Crowded with retailers and franchise
  handyman brands. Winnable over time on installer-led, labor-focused content, not
  quickly.
- **Handyman in Spokane.** Dominated by two franchises (Mr. Handyman, Handyman
  Connection) and directories. A long game; the Google Business Profile will do
  more here than any page.

## What is already built in

- Unique title, description, canonical and share image on every page; one H1 each
- `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList` and
  `ContactPage` structured data, built only from verified fields
- No review or rating markup, and no hours, address or credentials in schema
  until those facts are confirmed
- XML sitemap and robots.txt that follow the data: unpublished services and empty
  Reviews/Projects pages are left out automatically
- Each service page answers the real questions: what it involves, what drives
  cost, the process, what to send, honest limits, FAQs
- Internal links between related services, and from the homepage, services index,
  landlords page and service-areas page
- Static pages, optimized images with fixed dimensions, almost no client JavaScript

## Months 1 to 2: measure and claim

- Launch, then connect Search Console and GA4. Submit the sitemap.
- Top up the keyword tool and pull real Spokane volumes for the map above. Reorder
  priorities by what is actually searched.
- Google Business Profile, done properly: correct primary category, every service
  listed, service areas matching the site, 10+ real job photos, the website link.
  For a service-area business this drives more calls than the website.
- Make name, phone and website identical everywhere: GBP, Facebook, Nextdoor,
  Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, BBB, Angi/Thumbtack if used.
- Start asking every happy customer for a Google review, with the short link.

## Months 3 to 4: prove the specialty

- Publish the first real projects, carpet repairs first. Each one is a photo pair
  plus three or four sentences: the problem, what was done, the neighborhood.
- Add real reviews to the site as they come in.
- Check Search Console for queries with impressions but poor position, and tighten
  the matching page's title and opening lines.
- Post each finished job to the Google Business Profile.

## Months 5 to 8: widen carefully

- **City pages, only where earned.** Spokane Valley first, then Liberty Lake,
  once there are real jobs, photos and at least one review from that area to put
  on the page. No copy-and-swap-the-city pages; those get ignored or penalised.
- Add the questions customers actually ask as short guides, for example: "Can
  carpet be patched without leftover carpet?", "Why does carpet ripple?", "Carpet
  repair or replacement: how to decide", "What does LVP installation involve?".
- Build local links the honest way: realtors and property managers Matt works
  with, suppliers, neighborhood associations, local sponsorships.
- If landlord work is growing, split `/for-property-managers` into a landlord
  page and a realtor page with their own examples.

## Months 9 to 12: compound

- Review what the data says and double down: more projects and guides for the
  services that convert, fewer resources on the ones that do not.
- Refresh service pages with real figures Matt is comfortable publishing, such as
  typical job sizes or timeframes, and real photos in place of every stock image.
- Consider a small Local Services Ads or Google Ads test on the carpet repair
  terms once the site converts reliably, using the tracked estimate form.
- Re-run the full site check and a fresh look at competitors' pages.

## How to tell if it is working

Track monthly, in this order of importance: calls and form requests, Google
Business Profile calls and direction requests, impressions and clicks for the
mapped terms in Search Console, number of reviews. Rankings are a means; booked
jobs are the measure.
